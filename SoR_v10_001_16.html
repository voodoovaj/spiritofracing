What Is This?
Spirit of Racing is a single-file HTML motorsport career tracker. Record your race results from a sim racing game and SoR turns them into a living championship — standings, qualifying grids, driver ratings, team dynamics, and (with an API key) a full AI-driven career narrative with emails, goals, and paddock drama.

Everything runs in your browser. No internet required for the core tracker. No account, no server, no installation.
Getting Started
1. Open the HTML file in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
2. On the splash screen, select a season and click Enter Season
3. Go to the Career tab — set your Player Name and Driver Slot (the driver whose seat you occupy)
4. Go to Races, open Round 1, and hit Start ▶ to begin

Your save data lives in the browser's localStorage. Use ⬇ Export regularly to back up as a JSON file.

No API Key Required

⭐ Driver Ratings — performance grades (F through A+) built from grid vs finish data across all seasons

📊 Championship Probability — win scenarios and odds for top drivers
👤 Career page — personal stats, team panel, transfer assessment
😈 Relationships — emoji indicators in standings, auto-updated after each race
📋 Championship History — permanent record of every completed season's champions
🗓 Season Management — create, copy, rename, reset, delete seasons
✏️ Roster Editor — drivers, teams, tiers, calendar, points scale
💾 Export / Import — full save backup and restore as JSON
Requires API Key (Career Mode)
📬 Post-race inbox — color-coded emails from crew chief, personal assistant, press, and stewards
🎯 Goals — automatically calculated each round; referenced in the email
🔁 Transfer window — contract offers at season boundaries
📋 Season briefing and debrief — narrative arc across the full season
🤝 Former teammates — drivers you've raced with appear as characters in future seasons
Your Driver Slot
You occupy one driver's seat in the roster. That driver becomes inactive — shown with a 🔴 red dot in the team panel. They still exist in the paddock and may appear in narrative, but they no longer score points.

Your personal points are tracked separately. They travel with you between teams — each team only gets constructor credit for what you score while driving for them.

Active teammates show a 🟢 green dot. The inactive slot driver shows their pre-takeover points for historical reference.
Races & Qualifying
Each round shows a race report card when you first click it, including your current goal if one has been set. Hit Start ▶ to reveal the grid and enter results.

Qualifying grid is generated dynamically each round based on recent form, championship standing, team strength, and driver reputation built up across seasons. Round 1 is more tier-driven since there's no race history yet.

Weather is shown as a label (Sunny → Storm). Dry conditions are more common than wet.

⚡ Simulate Results auto-fills positions based on qualifying order with noise — useful for rounds you want to move through quickly.
Driver Ratings
The Rating tab in Standings shows a performance grade for every driver, built from their actual history across all seasons — comparing where they started on the grid to where they finished.

Grades run from F through A, with an A+ badge reserved for drivers who finished in the top 3 of any completed championship. A+ drivers carry a permanent qualifying edge across all future seasons.

Ratings marked with a * are provisional — fewer than 5 races in the data. Give it time before reading too much into them.
Relationships
Every driver has a relationship level with you, shown as an emoji in the standings table. Click any emoji to see the label and adjust it manually with ± buttons.

😈 Rival — hostile, cold, pointed. Never cordial.
😤 Tense — clipped, guarded, competitive
😐 Neutral
🙂 Friendly — warmer than average
😊 Ally — genuine support, may share information

Relationships shift automatically after each race based on on-track events. They carry over between seasons — grudges fade but don't disappear entirely. These levels are mandatory tone modifiers for the AI: a Rival will never write a friendly email.
Inbox (Career Mode + API Key)
Goals are calculated automatically the moment you save a round — no API call needed. The goal appears on your briefing card immediately and is handed to the AI when you check messages.

Check messages generates the full email batch — the button pulses gold when a new batch is ready. Each email is color-coded by sender type:

Purple — Teammate
Green — Crew Chief / Personal Assistant
Silver — Press / Journalists
Amber — Sponsors
Red — Rivals / Former teams / Former teammates
Grey — Stewards / Race Control (PENALTY badge)

The Crew Chief travels with you across all teams and seasons. Stefano knows what's happening in the paddock — driver tensions, personal conflicts, who's cracking under pressure. The Personal Assistant also travels with you — Andrea fabricates the story behind the story. Off-track drama, personal lives bleeding into performance, the gossip nobody else will say out loud.
Championship History
The History page records every completed season permanently — driver champion, constructor champion, and a running trophy count at the top. Records are never reset, even if you reset or delete the season itself.

Your wins are highlighted in gold. AI winners appear in red. The page holds up to 100 season entries; totals accumulate without limit.
AI Cost (Career Mode)
Career Mode uses the Claude Haiku model via the Anthropic API — fast and cost-effective for narrative generation. You are billed directly by Anthropic based on your usage; SoR does not charge anything.

Goals, relationship updates, and grid generation are all calculated locally with no API call.
Season Management
Reset Scores — zeros all race results and grid locks. Keeps inbox, goals, and career data. Also clears briefing flags so the Start button reappears for every round.

Start Over — full wipe including personal scores, inbox, goals, and narrative. Completely fresh.

Team Tiers — set in the Roster editor (1 = fastest, 10 = slowest). A wide spread produces more realistic grids. Bunching teams in the 1–3 range makes grids unpredictable.

Saving Your Game
SoR uses browser localStorage — data persists between sessions automatically.

⚠️ localStorage is browser and device specific. Clearing browser data will erase your save. Export regularly.

⬇ Export — downloads a dated JSON backup of everything.
⬆ Import — restores from a JSON file, replacing all current data.

Recommended: export after every session. Keep 2–3 recent backups.
Tips
🏎 Use a wide tier spread. Try 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 instead of clustering teams — it produces grids that reflect real mid-field gaps.

📬 The narrative knows your standings. The AI reads your actual points, championship position, nearby rivals, and constructor battle. It will not invent historical results.

😈 Let relationships develop. They shift automatically based on racing. Manual nudges are available if you want to make peace — or escalate a grudge.

⭐ Driver ratings improve over time. The rating system draws on grid vs finish data across all your seasons. A driver who consistently overperforms their car will earn a better qualifying spot — even in a new season.

💾 Export before big changes. Before resetting, switching seasons, or editing the roster, export your save as a backup.
